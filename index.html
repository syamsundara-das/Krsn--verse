Kṛṣṇa — The Supreme Personality of Godhead

This Kṛṣṇa Book presents the transcendental activities and pastimes of Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa, as explained through the teachings of the Vedic literature and the disciplic succession.

As we read, the purpose is not simply to finish chapters. We can hear about Kṛṣṇa with attention, remember His qualities and pastimes, and gradually develop our attraction to hearing and chanting His holy name.

Read slowly, try to remember the important events, and revisit chapters whenever necessary.
